Safety

Clear expectations for hands-on science.

Happy Science Calgary is a small pilot program. Activities are planned to be age-appropriate, supervised, and practical for kids ages 7-12.

Supervised activities

Kids follow guided steps, use age-appropriate materials, and work in a small pilot group of 8-12 kids per session.

Goggles when appropriate

Safety goggles are provided for activities where eye protection makes sense.

Mess-friendly clothing

Kids may get messy and should wear clothes suitable for hands-on activities.

Parent contact required

Parents should share allergies, support needs, and contact details before a child joins a session. Exact location is shared after registration/contact.

Questions

Ask before joining a pilot session.

Contact Eugene if you want to talk through materials, mess level, allergies, or whether a theme is a good fit.
