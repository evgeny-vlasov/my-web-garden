Privacy principles

Sharing is optional and deliberate.

Minimal identity

Use a first name or nickname only. A shared project does not need a full legal name, school, or personal contact information.

Images require permission

No identifiable photo without explicit parent permission. A screenshot or project-only image may often explain the work better.

Parents approve posting

A gallery entry requires parent permission and a review of what the linked project file reveals.

Students can decline

A direct in-class or family demonstration can complete the learning cycle without any gallery entry.
