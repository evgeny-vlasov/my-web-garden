Practical questions

What is the machine doing, and how would we know?

Students can compare prompts, inspect patterns, test outputs, collect confident mistakes, and distinguish a useful prediction from understanding.

The central habit is verification: what evidence supports the output, what may be missing, who could be affected, and what a person remains responsible for deciding.

Class and Lab direction

Use an output. Then try to break the claim.

A class would set a bounded question, establish privacy rules, inspect examples, and compare results. Lab work could design harder tests, classify failures, revise a prompt, or connect a limited prediction to a game or robot rule.

Fluent language is not evidence. A confident answer can still be wrong.

Human judgment remains responsible.

Any future AI work will state which tools are used, what data they receive, what claims are being tested, and which decisions remain with a person.
