For Parents

Practical answers before you choose a class.

School of Code is small, project-based, and serious about making learning visible. Some operating details are still being finalized, so we say that plainly instead of filling gaps with promises.

Discuss Fit With Eugene
Is this just more screen time?
No. Students are expected to make decisions, predict results, build, test, debug, explain, and improve. The computer is a construction material, not a stream of content to consume.
Does my child need experience?
No programming experience is required for a suitable beginner starting point. Previous experience is useful because projects and challenge depth can change. Contact us to discuss fit.
What ages are suitable?
Exact age ranges have not been finalized for publication. Readiness depends on the program, the student's interests, and comfort working through a problem. Contact us to discuss fit.
What device is needed?
Device arrangements have not been finalized for all formats. Requirements may differ between Scratch and robotics work. We will confirm current expectations before registration.
What is the difference between class and lab?
Class follows a planned learning path and introduces ideas through guided work. Computer Lab is additional guided project time to finish, modify, test, rebuild, debug, or prepare a demonstration.
What does a student take away from a game project?
A visible program plus the reasoning underneath it: coordinates, conditions, variables, state, debugging, design choices, and the ability to explain how the system behaves.
What happens when a student gets stuck?
Getting stuck is part of the work. The instructor helps reduce the problem, identify evidence, form a prediction, and choose a useful test without simply taking over the project.
What if my child is advanced?
The same concept can support a harder system, a stricter constraint, a deeper debugging task, or a more independent Lab Project. Placement and challenge level should be discussed before joining.
How large are the groups?
School of Code is designed around small groups so the instructor can understand each student's actual project. A final published maximum has not yet been set.
Are Roblox and AI available now?
No. Scratch & Game Design and Robotics are the active first programs. Roblox Studio / Lua and AI & Smart Machines are marked Available Later.
How will student work be shared safely?
Students can demonstrate work directly to families. Any future public gallery or blog should use clear family permission, avoid unnecessary personal information, and separate a student's identity from public project files. Public sharing is not assumed.
A finished small project beats a perfect unfinished one.

Finishing does not mean a project has no bugs or room to grow. It means the student has reached a version that works well enough to show, explain, and use as the starting point for the next question.
