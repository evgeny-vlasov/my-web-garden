Choosing a starting point

Choose the starting point that fits the student.

Scratch gives new programmers quick access to working code. Robotics suits students who want to connect instructions to inputs, outputs, and physical behaviour.

Previous experience usually changes the challenge, not the need to understand the basics. Ask Eugene about fit; current age ranges and group arrangements are discussed directly.
