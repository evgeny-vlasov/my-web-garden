CORE_IDEA

Coordinates describe position.

A screen is a map made from numbers. Every sprite, object, button, robot marker, game character, and target needs a way to answer:

Where am I?

Read the screen

Two number lines make a map.

x means left and right. y means down and up. Together, they name a position.

The centre is the origin: x: 0, y: 0. Positive x moves right, negative x moves left, positive y moves up, and negative y moves down.

  • x: -150, y: 110
  • x: 140, y: 120
  • x: -120, y: -100
  • x: 160, y: -90

Why it matters

The same idea returns in larger systems.

This first lesson does not teach every advanced application. It establishes a position model that students will meet again.
