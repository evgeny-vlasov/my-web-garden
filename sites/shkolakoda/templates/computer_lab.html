School of Code / Computer Lab

The Computer Lab supports the school's learning path.

Classes introduce ideas in a planned sequence. The Computer Lab gives students guided time to use those ideas more deeply, follow a problem, and make a project their own.

Structured freedom

Enough direction to keep moving. Enough room to invent.

CLASSES = PLANNED LEARNING PATH

Learn the idea

A topic, explanation, small test, guided build, and clear learning goal. Lessons connect so knowledge grows as a web rather than a pile of tricks.

COMPUTER LAB = GUIDED PROJECT TIME

Give it a life

Make a choice, try a strange feature, diagnose a failure, rebuild a weak section, or prepare the project for a demonstration.

What students may do

A useful menu of next moves.

Students do not need to wait for a new assignment when the current project still has questions inside it.

  • Finish a class project
  • Rebuild it differently
  • Add a feature
  • Test a strange idea
  • Repair a bug
  • Work through a challenge card
  • Prepare something to demonstrate

Instructor role

Help without taking over the keyboard.

The instructor can narrow the problem, ask for a prediction, point to evidence, suggest a test, or offer a challenge card. The student still makes the consequential choices and explains the result.

A bug is information. Lab time gives students enough breathing room to read it.

What the Computer Lab is not Random gaming, unsupervised browsing, or unstructured device time. Students arrive to build, test, debug, change, explain, or improve something connected to the School of Code program.

The class gives the idea. The lab gives the idea a life.
