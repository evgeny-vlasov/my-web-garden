School of Code / Computer Lab

The Computer Lab supports the school's learning path.

Classes introduce ideas in a planned sequence. The Computer Lab gives students guided time to use those ideas more deeply, follow a problem, and make a project their own.

What students may do

A useful menu of next moves.

Students do not need a new assignment while the current project still has questions inside it.

  • Finish a class project
  • Rebuild it differently
  • Add a feature
  • Test a strange idea
  • Repair a bug
  • Follow a challenge card
  • Prepare a demonstration
  • Help another student after trying independently

Instructor role

Help without taking over the keyboard.

The instructor can narrow the problem, ask for a prediction, point to evidence, suggest a test, or offer a challenge card. The student still makes consequential choices and explains the result.

A bug is information. Lab time gives students enough breathing room to read it.

What the Computer Lab is notRandom gaming, passive video watching, uncontrolled browsing, or unstructured device time. Students arrive to build, test, debug, change, explain, improve, or demonstrate something connected to the School of Code program.
