Why it belongs

A game world can become a reason to learn text-based code.

Students who understand events, conditions, variables, and game state in Scratch can meet those ideas again in Lua. The syntax changes; the underlying questions remain.

Roblox adds 3D coordinates, object hierarchies, properties, reusable functions, simple NPC behaviour, and the responsibility of making rules understandable to other players.

Class and Lab direction

Learn the rule together. Build a different world around it.

A class would introduce one Lua structure in a guided system. Lab work could change the level, combine challenges, test fairness, or repair a state bug.

The interesting part is the system the student writes.

Original rules, readable scripts, responsible assets, and tests with other players turn a familiar game platform into programming work.
