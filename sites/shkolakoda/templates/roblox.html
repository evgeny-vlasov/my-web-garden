This page explains where Roblox fits in the School of Code direction. It is not an announcement of current classes or registration.

Why it belongs

A game world can become a reason to learn text-based code.

Students who understand events, conditions, variables, and game state in Scratch can meet those ideas again in Lua. The syntax changes; the underlying questions remain.

Roblox also introduces 3D coordinates, object hierarchies, reusable functions, client-server thinking, and the responsibility of building for other players.

Future project direction

Systems, not just decoration.

PROJECT_01

Disappearing Platform Factory

Use events, timing, and state to build platforms that behave predictably even when the level does not.

PROJECT_02

Museum of Bad Traps

Script several deliberately flawed obstacles, test them, and explain exactly why each one fails.

PROJECT_03

NPC With One Terrible Idea

Build a character with a small state machine, clear triggers, and behaviour players can understand.
