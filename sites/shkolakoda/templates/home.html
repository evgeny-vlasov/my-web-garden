What a day looks like

A simple rhythm that keeps kids engaged.

Each day balances guided experiments, active breaks, creative problem solving, and time to explain what happened in kid-friendly language.

Welcome & safety setup Names, parent contact check, expectations, and goggles when appropriate.
Experiment block Hands-on activities with clear steps and room for curiosity.
Snack and outdoor reset Short breaks help kids stay focused and comfortable.
Build, test, improve Kids compare results, adjust ideas, and try again.
Wrap-up Take-home recap so parents know what their child explored.

Safety & parent trust

Designed for curious kids and practical parents.

Safe materials first

Activities are planned around age-appropriate materials, clear supervision, and safety goggles when appropriate.

Small pilot groups

Each session is capped at 8-12 kids so the group stays manageable.

Outdoor/backyard format

The pilot format uses open, flexible space for messy experiments and movement. Kids should wear clothes suitable for hands-on activities.

No overpromising

This is a fun enrichment day, not a school replacement or advanced tutoring program.
