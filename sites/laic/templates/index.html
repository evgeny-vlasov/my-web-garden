What we help with

Small AI tools that solve concrete operational problems

Front-door support

Website FAQ bots, intake helpers, and service navigation tools that help people find the right next step.

Internal staff support

Search across policies, shared documents, and program notes so small teams spend less time digging for answers.

Reporting and admin help

Summaries, drafts, spreadsheet cleanup, and lightweight automations that reduce repetitive work.

Who it is for

Organizations doing real work with limited time and budget

We focus on nonprofits, local initiatives, community groups, and service organizations that need practical help, not a giant platform rollout.

That includes teams dealing with messy data, busy staff, and urgent service work.

How a microproject works

Start small, test quickly, keep human review in place

1

Pick one problem

Choose a real bottleneck: repeated questions, messy files, intake triage, or staff search.

2

Build a bounded prototype

Create a small tool or workflow in days or weeks, not a year-long platform.

3

Review and refine

Test usefulness, privacy fit, and staff trust before expanding anything further.
