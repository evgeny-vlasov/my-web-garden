For nonprofits

Built for small teams doing urgent work

We understand limited budgets, messy data, competing priorities, and the need to avoid unnecessary complexity.

Start with one small problem

A narrow, useful tool is usually better than a broad transformation plan. We look for projects that can become useful in days or weeks, not a giant platform rollout.

Keep privacy and human review in mind

AI systems can be helpful and still require careful review. Sensitive data, public-facing responses, and staff decisions need clear boundaries.

No pressure to adopt AI where it does not help

If a regular form, spreadsheet cleanup, or process fix is the better answer, that is still useful progress.

Affordable, prototype-first work

The goal is to make experimentation possible for local organizations that do not have enterprise budgets or in-house AI teams.
