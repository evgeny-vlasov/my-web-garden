Microprojects

Small, bounded AI projects

A microproject is a focused AI tool or workflow improvement built around one problem, one audience, and a short timeline.

FAQ bot trained on public website content

Helps visitors get quick answers without replacing the website itself.

Volunteer onboarding assistant

Makes role expectations, orientation materials, and common questions easier to navigate.

Donation or grant report helper

Drafts summaries and highlights from existing notes and metrics.

Service navigation chatbot

Supports people trying to figure out which program, service, or resource fits their situation.

Spreadsheet cleanup and reporting helper

Improves consistency and reporting from exported operational data.

Internal policy and document search

Lets staff ask plain-language questions against approved internal materials.

Client inquiry triage

Organizes common inquiry types so staff can respond more quickly and consistently.
