    About

    A small independent pool repair service

    PoolEmergency is built around practical hands-on repair work for PVC and vinyl pool liner problems, especially small leaks, patches, and minor damage.

    Focused on repairs, not big construction

    This is a small local service for pool owners who need a direct answer about a liner leak, tear, puncture, or small PVC repair. The goal is to look at the actual damage and decide what is realistic.

    A hot air welding tool is available for suitable PVC and vinyl repairs. Whether welding, patching, or a temporary repair is appropriate depends on the material, access, surface condition, water level, and where the damage sits.

    No exaggerated guarantees and no pretending every problem has a quick fix. If the liner is too worn or the damage is in the wrong place, the honest answer may be that repair is limited or not worth doing.

    What to expect

    • Plain language about what can be repaired
    • Small-job mindset and practical fixes
    • Hot air welding when suitable for the material
    • Advice before big promises
    • Phone-first contact for urgent issues
