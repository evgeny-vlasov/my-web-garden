    Practical pool liner repair notes

    Short, non-technical notes for small PVC and vinyl pool liner problems.

    What to do when you find a small pool leak

    Try to note where the water level stops dropping and whether the leak changes when the pump is running. Avoid scrubbing or stretching the damaged area until it has been looked at. If the leak is active, call or text and describe where you see water loss, bubbles, wrinkles, or damage.

    When a vinyl patch can work

    A patch has the best chance when the liner still has some flexibility, the damaged area is reachable, and the surface can be cleaned. Patches are less reliable on brittle, badly aged, oily, or heavily stretched material. The location matters as much as the size of the hole.

    Why clean and dry surfaces matter for PVC repairs

    Most repair methods need a clean surface to bond or weld properly. Dirt, algae, sunscreen residue, moisture, and loose material can all weaken a repair. Some wet or underwater temporary patches may help slow a leak, but a cleaner repair area usually gives a better result.
