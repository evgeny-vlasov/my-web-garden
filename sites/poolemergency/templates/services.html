    Services

    Small pool liner repairs and practical advice

    PoolEmergency focuses on smaller PVC and vinyl repair work: leaks, patches, minor damage, and repair decisions that depend on the real condition of the pool.

    What I can help with

    • Small pool liner leaks
    • PVC and vinyl patches
    • Minor tears, cuts, and punctures
    • Seam repairs where the material and location make repair reasonable
    • Emergency temporary patches to slow water loss
    • Inspection and repair advice before you spend more than needed
    • Small pool-related PVC repairs using hot air welding when suitable

    What may not be repairable

    Not every liner problem is a good candidate for repair. A patch or weld may not hold if the liner is brittle, stretched, dirty, badly aged, inaccessible, under heavy tension, or damaged in a difficult location.

    Large liner failures, major structural pool work, full liner replacement, and construction projects are outside the normal scope of this small repair service.

    How a visit works

    1

    Describe the problem

    Call, text, or use the form. Include your neighbourhood, pool type if known, and what changed.

    2

    Assess access and material

    The damage needs to be reachable, cleanable, and made of material that can accept the repair method.

    3

    Repair if it makes sense

    If a practical repair is reasonable, the work is kept focused. If not, you get a clear explanation.
