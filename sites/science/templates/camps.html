Camps

Pilot weekend camp themes for curious kids.

Our first pilot camps are single-day weekend sessions for kids ages 7-12. Each pilot session keeps the group small, the science supervised and hands-on, and the parent expectations clear.

Pilot camp cards

Choose a theme that fits your child's curiosity.

💥

Mad Science Day

Age range: 7-12

Duration: Weekend day session

What kids do: Explore foam, fizz, colour change, simple reactions, and supervised demonstrations that make chemistry visible.

What they take home: A short experiment recap, vocabulary from the day, and a small take-home activity or observation prompt.

Parent note: This theme can be messy. Materials are chosen for age-appropriate supervised activities, goggles are provided when appropriate, and cleanup is built into the plan.

🦠

Micro Monsters & Biology

Age range: 7-12

Duration: Weekend day session

What kids do: Practise observation skills, compare living and non-living samples, learn beginner cell ideas, and investigate tiny worlds.

What they take home: A mini science notebook page, observation drawings, and simple questions to continue noticing biology at home.

Parent note: Activities are planned to be age-appropriate, supervised, and grounded in beginner-friendly biology.

🫧

Slime Lab

Age range: 7-12

Duration: Weekend day session

What kids do: Mix, stretch, compare textures, test small changes, and learn how ingredients affect the final material.

What they take home: Their slime sample in a container, a care note, and a simple explanation of the experiment variables.

Parent note: This is one of the messier options. Kids should wear clothes suitable for hands-on activities. We use supervised steps, clear boundaries, and cleanup routines.

🕵

Spy Science Lab

Age range: 7-12

Duration: Weekend day session

What kids do: Use clues, codes, fingerprints, invisible ink, and logic to work through a low-pressure science mystery.

What they take home: A detective-style lab sheet, a code challenge, and a recap of the science tools they used.

Parent note: This theme is active but less messy than slime or reaction days, with a focus on careful observation, teamwork, and parent contact for each child.

Pilot info

Pilot price: $76 per child / day.

Capacity is limited to 8-12 kids per session while the program is being tested and refined. Exact pilot dates and location details will be shared after registration/contact.
