Welcome

I'm dedicated to providing a safe, supportive environment where you can explore your thoughts and feelings, develop coping strategies, and work toward positive change.

With 25 years of experience in psychotherapy, I understand that seeking help is a courageous step. My approach is collaborative and client-centered, focusing on your unique needs, strengths, and goals. Together, we will explore the challenges you are experiencing and I will support you in finding effective solutions, while building practical tools and strategies for healing and personal growth.

I hope that our therapeutic relationship will facilitate change. My practice is grounded in respect, empathy, and care for the person I work with. Whether you're dealing with anxiety, depression, life transitions, or seeking personal growth, I'm here to support you in your journey.

Therapeutic Approach

Client-Centered & Collaborative

I believe you are the expert in your own life. My role is to offer a supportive, non-judgmental environment where you can freely explore your experiences and discover your unique path forward. Psychotherapy is a collaborative journey; together, we will identify your goals and develop strategies tailored to your needs.

Evidence-Based Methods

My practice integrates various evidence-based therapeutic approaches, tailored to meet your individual needs:

My guiding paradigm is Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT). This approach seeks to identify patterns of thinking and behavior that sustain negative and harmful life patterns. I also use a variety of methods, including Cognitive-Behavioral Coaching, Neuro-linguistic Programming, Narrative Therapy, Transactional Analysis, and Positive Psychotherapy and Mindfulness-Based Approaches.

Areas of Specialization

I have particular experience working with:

  • Anxiety, Anger and Stress: Generalized anxiety, panic attacks, social anxiety, stress and anger
  • Depression & Mood Concerns: Low mood, lack of motivation, and emotional difficulties in general
  • Relationship Issues: Communication, conflict resolution, boundary setting
  • Work-Life Balance: Burnout, professional stress, work-related challenges
  • Personal Growth: Self-esteem, identity exploration, goal setting, strategic thinking
  • Also I work with people who have issues with the law (domestic violence, drinking and driving, theft under $5000, voyeurism, sexual harassment, and other problems)
